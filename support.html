Juncture support

Juncture suggests what to train, when, and where, from the gaps in your calendar and how recovered you are. If something is not working, or you want to tell us what you think, email patfoley4291@gmail.com.

For a bug, the fastest way to help us is Settings › Diagnostics › Share status inside the app: it copies a short, plain-text description of what the app could see (which permissions were granted, what connected, what it decided). Paste it into the email.

Common questions

It says "worked example". Nothing is connected yet. Connect Apple Health and a calendar in Settings and every screen switches to your own data.

No Apple Watch? That is fine. The phone's own step and exercise data counts, and sleep from any app that writes to Apple Health.

A gym shows no timetable. Timetables are published for a growing set of studios in Austin and New York. Everywhere else, the venue's phone, website and directions are what the app can offer.

Where does my data go? Nowhere. The privacy policy says exactly what is read and what leaves the phone.
